## Included versions and modifications

- All six unique songs from Funk (Collection) are included. The archive’s duplicate OGG versions are omitted.
- Pure Raceway uses the standard 160 BPM version; Hot Roadway uses its standard 160 BPM version. The optional Climax variants are omitted.
- All tracks were converted to 44.1 kHz stereo MP3 at 160 kbps with FFmpeg/libmp3lame to reduce download size and support common browsers.
- Two-pass loudness normalization targets −18 LUFS, a −1.5 dBTP true-peak ceiling, and an 11 LU loudness range, with linear normalization requested. The complete musical content is retained: no excerpts, remixes, fades, or tempo changes were made.
- Source, artist, license, and transformation notes are also embedded in each MP3’s metadata.
